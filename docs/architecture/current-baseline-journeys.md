## Measurement boundaries

[Terminal/tab samples](current-baseline-performance.json) include 200 samples for idle output, 200 with a 4-KiB output burst, and 200 actual selected-tab changes. Output timing starts at renderer IPC dispatch and ends at the matching PTY event; two subsequent animation frames are also recorded. An additional 200 Playwright keyboard inputs traverse the focused xterm and raw-mode /bin/cat: renderer keydown to echoed PTY output has p95 0.8 ms, and keydown to two subsequent animation frames has p95 15.6 ms. Physical display latency and xterm paint completion are not measured. Thus these figures cannot certify the specification's complete input-feedback target.

[Startup samples](current-baseline-startup.json) use ten new processes and fresh profiles. OS caches were not flushed. Resource samples separate Electron processes from the owned daemon; model services are excluded. A separate configured four-agent run records owned app/daemon descendants after ten seconds without submitted queries; ps CPU is lifetime average, not interval CPU. [Power/thermal observation](current-baseline-environment.json) was captured afterward, not continuously during the run.

Task 01 records this baseline and its limitations; full journey completion remains with the owning tasks. Do not treat this matrix as six passing end-to-end journeys.
